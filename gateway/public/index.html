Stop AI agents from paying impostors.

Any x402 seller can copy another agent's name, card and badge and put its own wallet in payTo. VeriFlow checks every payment against Concordium before the buyer signs: the seller must be an Active agent in the on-chain Agent Registry, owned by an identity-verified person, and the money must go to that agent's own registered wallet. On mainnet today, 1 in 4 Active agents (1,269 of 5,393) could not be paid under this rule, mostly because they never bound a wallet on-chain (1,114): a readiness gap, not a count of impostors (census).

Why Concordium: every agent owner is a verified identity; a buyer can prove "18+, allowed country" in zero knowledge; and a smart contract can read the Agent Registry inside the payment itself. VeriFlow uses all three. The BTC/ETH call sold here is only a sample product: the checks are the product, shipped as two npm packages and an escrow contract.

Before delivering

Is this buyer allowed?

A Verify & Access zero-knowledge proof (18+, allowed country) that reveals nothing else, re-checked by the seller at every payment.

Afterwards

Was what I bought changed later?

Every call is committed on-chain before its outcome, one per asset and day, under a scoring rule registered first. Your browser checks it.

Try 1Watch an agent buy one call

Our buyer agent #1478 runs on this server. It checks this seller, agent #1313, on Concordium before signing anything, pays 1 testnet CCD over x402, and the seller checks it back. You pay nothing. Then your browser checks the call it bought against the chain.

Try 2Buy a BTC call through the escrow contract

The same buyer buys a BTC call, but the money goes through our escrow contract instead of a direct transfer, so the chain, not our server, decides whether it reaches agent #1313. The seller quotes a memo bound to this buyer (whose zero-knowledge proof is re-checked first); the signal is handed over only after the contract has paid, read back from the contract. The contract calls the CIS-8004 Agent Registry itself: when the CCD comes in (held only for an Active agent with an agentWallet, which it records), and again at release (paid only to that recorded wallet, only if the registry still names it). First it is asked about three agents it must refuse; those are read-only calls, nothing is sent. You pay and sign nothing.

This button is our own buyer paying our own seller. Done by someone else: a friend of the developer, on his own account (19836), paid #1313 into the escrow and never released it; after the window #1313's own wallet claimed it, the contract reading the registry again. And when a test agent's wallet was swapped after payment, the release was refused on-chain (−22) and the buyer refunded.

Try 3Check another team's seller before paying

The same checks our buyer runs, as a guard any x402 agent can wrap around its own client (npm install concordium-x402-guard). This server calls the seller, reads whom it wants to be paid, and checks that seller on Concordium. Nothing is paid. Results are cached for a minute. One runs on Concordium mainnet: x402 Doctor (#5120), checked live against the mainnet Agent Registry and its CIS-8 key binding by the published concordium-x402-guard package.

On-chain commitments

Since 2026-11-22 every call is committed to contract v2 <13115,0>: at most one per asset and day, accepted only in the day after that close, before any sale and long before the 7-day outcome it is scored on. Rows marked v1 come from the retired system (<13094,0>), which allowed more than one commit per day; that is why v2 replaced it. No hit rate is shown: the score is computed only from the chain and Binance by the scoring rule registered on-chain before the first v2 call. This page recomputes hashes in your browser but reads transactions through this server; for a check that does not, open the transaction on CCDExplorer.
