VeriFlow agent escrow v2 <13171,0> on Concordium testnet, 2026-11-27 (UTC). Run by the developer from gateway/ with `npm run escrow -- …`
(buyer: our agent #1478, account 19528). Output as printed. Every call is dry-run first; --send-anyway sends a call the contract
would reject, to leave the rejection on-chain as evidence (it costs the fee; no CCD moves).

Why v2: an outside review (Cursor) of v1 <13170,0> found that v1 paid whatever agentWallet the registry named at payout time, so an
agent owner could re-point the wallet after a payment and have it paid elsewhere; that a registry answer v1 could not read after
the window left the CCD stuck; and that there was no final deadline. v2 pays only the wallet recorded at payment, only while the
registry still names it for an Active agent (else -22 WalletChanged, and the buyer may refund); refuses a wallet that is not an
account (-23); leaves the refund open whenever the payout check fails; and lets the buyer refund in any case 30 days after the
window. Chain tests for each: contract-escrow/tests/chain.rs. v1 stays on chain, superseded; its evidence: escrow-2026-11-27.txt.
